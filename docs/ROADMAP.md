# Portland 2026 Trip App — Roadmap

Ideas for later. Nothing here is scheduled.

## App features
- **Editable packing list**, so Paula can add items on her phone (London had one; Victoria and Portland have a fixed list).
- **"Open in Waze" / Apple Maps** buttons next to Google Maps.
- **Drive-time check:** a "leave by" reminder on the Dolly Olive stop (10-minute grace period) and on the Eminent Domaine stop (70-minute visit).
- **Card-shop haul log:** a simple per-stop notes box saved on the phone.
- **Ticket viewer:** Victoria had a ticket/boarding-pass modal. It was left out here because there are no tickets; port it back if needed.
- **Shared template:** pull the common renderer out of london/victoria/portland so the next trip app starts from data only.
